About Us

About Our Studio

Excellence in every wash with GlowLuxe

Because your vehicle deserves more than automated tunnel washes and abrasive friction brushes, GlowLuxe was founded to deliver concours-level paint restoration, surgical micro-marring correction, and ultra-durable ceramic preservation under clean-room conditions.

We employ closed-loop deionized zero-TDS pure water filtration and bespoke polymer formulas that gently lift road film without degrading your factory clearcoat or bespoke carbon finishes.

  • Eco-Friendly Products

    Zero caustic acids, pure deionized water, and non-toxic polymer shampoos fully safe for exotic composite paints and rubber weatherstripping.

  • Expert Detailers

    IDA-certified master technicians with over 15 years specializing in precision clearcoat correction, high-gloss jeweling, and hand-applied ceramics.

Our Evolution

Effortless clean, exceptional care

The journey of precision automotive preservation, concours excellence, and client trust.

2018

The Beginning

Founded in a two-bay artisan studio dedicated exclusively to hand-correcting vintage European sports cars and exotic clearcoats with bespoke wool pads and custom-blended carnauba pastes.

2021

Expanding Our Services

Expanded into a state-of-the-art facility featuring climate-controlled 9H ceramic coating bays, paint protection film (PPF) plotting suites, and hypoallergenic interior dry-steam restoration.

2024

Sustainability Commitment

Achieved 100% closed-loop zero-effluent water recycling and launched our fully solar-powered rapid-curing bays, setting the national benchmark for sustainable luxury auto preservation.
